Logbook and your health data

Logbook is a training journal. With your permission it reads data that other apps, such as Google Health with a Fitbit, have saved in Health Connect on this phone.

What it reads

Heart rate and total calories burned during the workouts you log; sleep sessions and stages, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, breathing rate and daily steps; and workouts recorded by other apps, such as a bike ride.

What it does with it

It shows a heart-rate chart, calories, time in heart-rate zones and a training load next to each workout, uses them to shade the activity heatmap, and works out a daily training readiness score with a suggestion for the day's session.

Where it stays

On this phone, in Logbook's own storage, and in backup files that you export yourself. Logbook has no account, no servers and no analytics. Health data is never uploaded, shared or sold, and Logbook writes nothing to Health Connect.

Your control

You can turn heart-rate syncing off in Logbook's settings and remove its access at any time in Health Connect. Deleting a workout, or erasing all data in Logbook, deletes the copy kept with it.
